# Why do I want to watch a motivational Anime or manga?

So, recently I have again gone back to my quest of finding something as motivational and provides as good experience as I had when I watched Bakuman. Bakuman is an anime/manga written by the same mangaka who created Death Note. It is not as popular as many mainstream anime and manga out there. I cannot even find the dubbed version of this anime on the internet these days. Maybe there isn't at all. Then why do I love this anime so much and want to watch something similar to it? The reason is very simple. When I watch Bakuman for the first time, I felt so motivated to do something great in my life. Two kids can take the industry by storm and fulfill their dreams with such resolve. Then why can't I? I have so much experience with my growing age
